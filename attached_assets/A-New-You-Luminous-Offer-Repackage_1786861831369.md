# A New You — Luminous Leadership Offer Repackage

## Executive recommendation

The existing pages contain a credible transformational offer, but the buyer has to work too hard to understand it. The material repeatedly describes the philosophy, uses several overlapping proprietary terms and presents three offers without a clean decision path.

The new sales journey should answer five questions immediately:

1. Who is this for?
2. What real problem does it solve?
3. What will change?
4. What exactly do I receive?
5. Which option is right for me, and what does it cost?

The offer should be repositioned under one clear umbrella:

> **Luminous Leadership Transformation**  
> Resolve the patterns holding you back. Lead with clarity. Live with freedom.

This is not primarily a “membership”. It is a guided leadership and personal transformation pathway for purpose-driven business owners, Christian leaders and entrepreneurs. Calling it a membership makes a premium, facilitated program sound like a content subscription.

## What is currently making the offer difficult to buy

- The outcome is buried beneath abstract language.
- “Luminous Break Free Experience” is used for both group and private pathways.
- The 12-week private program and 12-month programs are not presented as a logical progression.
- Buyers cannot quickly distinguish format, access, time commitment or level of personal support.
- The pages alternate between Christian, spiritual, neuroscience, business, wellness and “quantum” language without explaining how these elements work together.
- Claims such as “identity-level transformation”, “subconscious re-patterning” and nervous-system change need careful, credible wording and should not imply guaranteed clinical outcomes.
- Pricing emphasises discounts before establishing value.
- “This Month’s Special” requires a real deadline. If the price is ongoing, call it a current introductory rate rather than manufacturing urgency.
- The tax-deduction statement should be softened to: “Professional-development expenses may be deductible depending on your circumstances. Please seek advice from your accountant.”
- The buyer is asked to “apply” without being told what happens after applying.

## Positioning foundations

### Primary audience

- Purpose-driven and Christian business owners
- Founders, entrepreneurs and senior leaders
- Coaches, creatives and wellness professionals
- Leaders experiencing a new season, plateau, recurring pattern or loss of clarity
- People who value faith, personal responsibility and whole-person development

### The problem in the buyer’s language

You have worked hard, learned the strategies and built something meaningful—but the same patterns may still appear: overthinking, self-doubt, people-pleasing, hesitation, burnout, strained relationships or difficulty stepping into the next level of leadership.

The problem is not always a lack of information or another business tactic. Sometimes the greatest constraint is an internal pattern you cannot clearly see from inside it.

### The promised transformation

The program helps participants:

- recognise recurring patterns and blind spots;
- understand how those patterns affect decisions, leadership and relationships;
- develop greater clarity and confidence;
- respond to pressure with more emotional steadiness;
- make decisions that align with their values and calling;
- improve communication, boundaries and trust;
- translate personal insight into practical action;
- sustain change through guided integration and ongoing support.

Avoid promising that every participant will increase profit, cure burnout, heal trauma or achieve permanent transformation. Outcomes depend on the participant and circumstances.

### 13. FAQ copy

## Frequently asked questions

### Is this business coaching, counselling or a course?

It is a facilitated leadership and personal-development program. It combines guided reflection, practical learning, faith-aligned principles and tools for integrating change. It is not medical treatment, psychotherapy or crisis support, and it does not replace care from an appropriately qualified health professional.

### Do I need to be a Christian?

The program openly draws on biblical principles and is designed to resonate strongly with Christian and spiritually grounded leaders. You should be comfortable with faith being part of the conversation. Clarify with Christine whether participation is open to people of other faiths before publishing this answer.

### How much time will I need?

The programs are designed for busy leaders. Your exact live-session schedule and recommended between-session practice will be provided before enrolment. Add concrete estimates here—for example, session duration and expected weekly practice—once confirmed.

### What is the difference between the three pathways?

The 12-week Intensive offers concentrated private work around a defined challenge. The 12-month Circle offers ongoing facilitated group development. The 12-month Signature Partnership includes the annual group journey plus private monthly facilitation and priority support.

### Are sessions online?

State the delivery method clearly. The current material indicates live online group sessions and private support, but the precise mix of Zoom, telephone and in-person delivery must be confirmed.

### What happens after I apply?

After submitting the short application, you will be invited to a private clarity conversation with Christine. You will discuss what you want to change, the kind of support you need and whether one of the three pathways is a suitable fit. There is no obligation to enrol.

### Are payment plans available?

Yes. Each pathway has a one-time payment and an instalment option. All prices are in Australian dollars. Display the total payable under each plan clearly.

### Can I undertake a program with someone else?

Shared options may be available for spouses, family members, business partners or colleagues. Contact Christine for suitability, format and personalised pricing.

### Can I claim the program as a business expense?

Professional-development expenses may be deductible depending on your circumstances. Please seek advice from your accountant; A New You does not provide tax advice.

### What results can I expect?

Every participant and situation is different. The program provides facilitation, tools, structure and support, but no specific personal, health or business result can be guaranteed. Your engagement and application are important parts of the process.

### Replace “special” pricing with one of these honest structures

- **Founding cohort rate** with a real close date and limited places;
- **Current enrolment rate** with no artificial countdown;
- **Pay-in-full saving** compared with the instalment total;

### Do not lead with tax or discounts

Lead with buyer fit, transformation, delivery and trust. Price comes after value is understandable. Tax information belongs in the FAQ, not near the headline.
